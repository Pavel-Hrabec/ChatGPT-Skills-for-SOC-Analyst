## Output format

### Summary
Answer the request directly.

### Result or requested artifact
Describe the observed commands and likely purpose.

### Evidence and reasoning
List the relevant command lines, process relationships, identities, and timestamps. Separate facts from inference.

### Assessment or validation status
Give a proportionate assessment and confidence, or state why the evidence is inconclusive.

### Unknowns and limitations
State missing command output, baselines, and context.

### Recommended next steps
Give prioritized, read-only checks for the process, host role, account, and follow-on activity.
